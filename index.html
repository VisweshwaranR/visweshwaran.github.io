About

I am a hydrologist working on data-driven methods for environmental and water systems, combining hydrological and groundwater modelling, data assimilation, GIS, and remote sensing. My work uses machine learning and mathematical frameworks to improve forecasting and our understanding of how these systems behave.

I am currently a Senior Postdoctoral Researcher at the Chair of Data-driven Computing in Civil Engineering, RWTH Aachen University, on the FLAIR project — developing an AI-based approach to predict ensembles of regional flood flows and inundation depth. Before Aachen I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Reading (2023–2026), where I carried out the first state–parameter assimilation for the JULES land surface model using hybrid En-Var assimilation of COSMOS-UK soil moisture observations, and assimilated streamflow into CaMa-Flood to improve UK flood inundation forecasts.

I hold a PhD in Remote Sensing from IIT Bombay, where my thesis developed efficient assimilation strategies for hydrological models. I review for Advances in Water Resources, Water, and the ISH Journal of Hydraulic Engineering, and I convene the Land Surface Data Assimilation Community of Practice.

My full curriculum vitae, with the complete list of publications, awards, and service, is available as a PDF download.
